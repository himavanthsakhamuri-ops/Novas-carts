What's really breaking NovaCart

Illustrative case data (fictional platform). Orders and revenue rise, so the dashboard looks healthy, but the growth is hiding a leak.

New orders+38%
Repeat purchase rate−21%
Cancellations+27%
Avg delivery time+9 min

Root cause (hypothesis)

Demand is bunched into the same 6–9 pm window, and the app promises one fixed ETA no matter how busy the dark stores are. Overload makes orders late, late orders get cancelled, and a customer who got a late order and no apology does not come back. Discounts buy new users, not trust.

To validate: split cancellations and repeats by order hour, promised vs actual ETA, and whether a late order got any recovery.

What this app does about it

1. Honest ETA + calm slots: shows live store load and rewards coins for choosing a quieter slot, flattening the peak.
2. Delay guarantee: late orders auto-issue a voucher, so a failure becomes a reason to return.
3. Play & streaks: daily games and spin earn vouchers, bringing people back between orders.
4. One-tap reorder: repeat purchases take seconds.

Impact to prove

Calm-slot share of orders · late-order rate · repeat rate within 14 days of a late order · voucher redemption.
